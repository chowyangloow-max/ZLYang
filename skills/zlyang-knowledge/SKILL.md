# ZLYang Knowledge

Turn validated work into durable knowledge without detaching it from evidence or lifecycle.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the source artifact passed audit, or record a time-bounded publication exception.
2. Define the knowledge unit, audience, problem solved, applicability, and non-applicability.
3. Attach evidence, upstream record IDs, current baseline, and audit verdict.
4. Add retrieval metadata: title, summary, tags, domain, and related assets.
5. Add owner, review cadence, freshness trigger, supersession links, and retirement criteria.
6. Publish as `candidate`, `published`, `stale`, `superseded`, or `retired`.

Do not publish context-free conclusions. A knowledge asset must say when it should not be used.

Read [knowledge rules](references/knowledge.md) for publication and lifecycle gates. Use [the unified model](../../docs/data-model.md) and `templates/knowledge.md`.

## Output

Produce a self-contained knowledge asset, provenance block, applicability boundary, maintenance metadata, and related-record links.
