# Knowledge publication rules

## Publication gate

A published asset needs a clear problem, audience, concise answer, applicability and non-applicability, upstream evidence, audit state, owner, review date or trigger, and replacement or retirement behavior.

## Freshness

Use time-based review for volatile domains and event-based review when laws, products, dependencies, policies, or baselines change. Mark an asset `stale` when its review boundary is crossed; do not silently keep it published as current.

## Granularity

Prefer one independently retrievable decision or procedure per asset. Link related assets instead of creating an unmaintainable omnibus document.

## Lifecycle

`candidate` to `published` to `stale` or `superseded` to `retired`. A superseded asset links to its replacement and remains available for historical traceability.
