# ZLYang Distill

Reduce information volume without reducing decision integrity.

## Workflow

1. Accept verified or explicitly qualified claims; do not silently promote unverified material.
2. Cluster exact duplicates, near-duplicates, dependencies, and contradictions.
3. Choose the strongest formulation by evidence and scope, not by rhetorical confidence.
4. Preserve load-bearing details and attach lineage to every distilled conclusion.
5. Separate settled conclusions, context, open conflicts, and discarded noise.
6. Record every merge, exclusion, compression, and restoration in the deletion ledger.
7. Update coverage destinations and stop when a material item has neither a retained destination nor a justified ledger entry.
8. Mark load-bearing ledger entries for independent review when required by the review plan.

Do not flatten different scopes into one rule. Do not resolve contradictions merely to make the output cleaner.

Read [distillation rules](references/distillation.md) before high-volume or conflict-heavy work. Use [the unified model](../../docs/data-model.md) and `templates/distillation.md`.

## Output

Produce the decision-ready core, protected details, conflict ledger, deletion ledger, coverage updates, lineage map, and handoff recommendation.
